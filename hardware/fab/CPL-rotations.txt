CPL rotation provenance
=======================

Mid X / Mid Y are millimetres from the board's bottom-left corner,
+Y up. Rotation is degrees CCW in the factory's frame: bottom-side parts
are mirrored as (180 - rotation) first, then the turn or correction
named on the part's line is added.

A part whose LCSC number has its land in the parts library is fitted
to that land, which is the factory's own: the board's pads are matched to
the land's by pad number (a diode's, an LED's or a polarised
capacitor's by which end is which, since the two libraries need not
number them alike), and the quarter turn and the centre that put
each pin on its own pad are written. Its Mid X / Mid Y is the land's
origin. Every other part takes the per-family table.
